# Animation Strategy

## Animation Principles

The animation should feel like luxury packaging being revealed — smooth, slow, tactile, and premium. Avoid fast, flashy, gimmicky, or gaming-style animation.

### Animation mood

- Slow reveal
- Soft parallax
- Premium hover
- Material depth
- Paper-like movement
- Subtle gold highlights
- Calm transitions

---

# Recommended Animations by Section

## 1. Header Animation

### Effect

Sticky header with subtle background blur and shadow on scroll.

### Look and feel

When the user scrolls down, the header gently changes from transparent/cream to a slightly frosted ivory surface with a soft border. The Request Quote button remains visible.

### Codex prompt

Create a sticky header animation using Framer Motion. At page top, the header should be transparent or very light. After scrollY passes 40px, animate to a warm ivory translucent background with backdrop blur, soft bottom border, and subtle shadow. Keep the Request Quote button visible. Use a 200ms ease-out transition. Ensure mobile header remains clean and accessible.

---

## 2. Hero Text Reveal

### Effect

Hero headline reveals line by line with slight upward motion and fade.

### Look and feel

The headline should feel like a premium editorial reveal, not a pop animation.

### Codex prompt

Animate the hero section using Framer Motion. Reveal the eyebrow first, then the hero headline line by line, then the subheadline, then CTA buttons. Each element should fade in and move upward by 16px with a stagger of 0.12s. Use easeOut and duration around 0.7s. Keep the animation subtle and premium.

---

## 3. Hero Product Parallax

### Effect

The product image moves very slightly slower than the scroll.

### Look and feel

The luxury packaging arrangement should feel cinematic, with very subtle depth.

### Codex prompt

Add a subtle hero image parallax using Framer Motion useScroll and useTransform. The hero background image should move vertically from 0px to 28px as the user scrolls through the hero. Add a very slight scale from 1.02 to 1.0. Keep the motion smooth and restrained. Do not distort the image or create aggressive movement.

---

## 4. CTA Button Hover

### Effect

Buttons lift slightly with gold glow or border enhancement.

### Look and feel

Premium and tactile, like pressing a luxury product detail.

### Codex prompt

Create reusable premium button hover states. On hover, the primary gold button should translateY(-2px), increase shadow subtly, and slightly deepen the gold tone. The secondary outline button should show a soft gold border glow and move up by 2px. Use CSS transitions of 180–220ms ease-out.

---

## 5. Trust Metrics Counter Reveal

### Effect

Metrics cards fade in and numbers count up.

### Look and feel

Confident but not flashy.

### Codex prompt

Create a TrustMetrics component. Animate each metric card on scroll into view with staggered fade-up motion. For numeric metrics like 2010, 500–10,000, 12+, and 9+, use a simple count-up animation triggered once when the section enters the viewport. Keep the count duration around 1 second and avoid excessive bouncing.

---

## 6. Product Card Hover

### Effect

Cards lift slightly; product image zooms subtly; gold action arrow moves.

### Look and feel

Refined product discovery.

### Codex prompt

Create product category cards with premium hover interactions. On hover, lift the card by 4px, increase shadow softly, scale the product image to 1.035, and move the CTA arrow 4px to the right. Add a thin gold border transition. Use CSS group-hover or Framer Motion whileHover. Keep all transitions smooth and subtle.

---

## 7. Rigid Box Formation Process Animation

### Effect

Process steps reveal sequentially from left to right.

### Look and feel

A production story unfolding step-by-step.

### Codex prompt

Create a RigidBoxProcess component with seven process steps: Greyboard Cutting, V-Grooving / Scoring, Corner Pasting, Frame Folding, Wrap Paper Printing, Wrapping & Turning In, Final Assembly & QC. Animate the steps on scroll so each step fades in and moves upward by 12px in sequence from left to right. Animate arrows between steps by scaling their width from 0 to 100% after the previous step appears. Add a subtle checkmark pulse on the final QC step. Use Framer Motion staggerChildren. On mobile, convert the process into a vertical timeline with the same reveal sequence.

---

## 8. Gallery Hover Animation

### Effect

Image cards reveal category label and title on hover.

### Look and feel

Like a premium portfolio gallery.

### Codex prompt

Build a gallery grid with image cards. On hover, slightly scale the image to 1.04, darken the image with a soft gradient overlay, and reveal the category and title with a fade-up animation. Keep the border radius and shadows consistent with the rest of the site. Avoid heavy overlays; the product image should remain visible.

---

## 9. Finishes & Materials Chips

### Effect

Chips gently appear in staggered sequence and glow on hover.

### Look and feel

A refined material selector.

### Codex prompt

Create a FinishesMaterials component with grouped chips for Surface, Luxury Detail, Structure, and Material. Animate chips into view with staggered fade-up motion. On hover, change border color to muted gold, background to warm ivory, and icon color to gold. Use small motion only; no bounce.

---

## 10. Quote CTA Section Animation

### Effect

Dark teal CTA band reveals with form sliding in from the right.

### Look and feel

A confident conversion close.

### Codex prompt

Animate the final quote CTA section. The left headline and copy should fade in from the left by 20px. The form card should fade in from the right by 24px with a slight delay. Use duration 0.7s and easeOut. Add gentle hover/focus states on inputs using muted gold borders and soft shadows.

---

## 11. Scroll Reveal System

### Effect

Every major section enters smoothly.

### Look and feel

Consistent premium rhythm.

### Codex prompt

Create a reusable Reveal component using Framer Motion and Intersection Observer behavior. It should accept children, delay, direction, and className props. Default animation: opacity 0 to 1, y 24px to 0, duration 0.7s, easeOut, trigger once when 20% of the element enters viewport. Use this component across section headings, cards, forms, and galleries for consistent premium scroll rhythm.
